**Draft:** Research proposal extracted from historical notes, not an approved position.

## Problem

The [historical foundational notes](https://github.com/Nirgalz/Anarchopeia/blob/0d8963af515be54957f767531017aff3d6881955/txt/principes%20fondateurs.txt) declare housing, safe food and water, education and freedom to develop activities to be basic conditions of a decent life. The [older equity discussion](https://github.com/Nirgalz/Anarchopeia/blob/0d8963af515be54957f767531017aff3d6881955/txt/puissance%20pouvoir%20et%20resolution.txt) distinguishes **identical allocation** from assistance adapted to different needs, including disability. Their designation as universally enforceable rights requires further institutional and legal design.

## Objectives

Investigate which essential goods and services should be assured to everyone and how effective access can accommodate unequal needs without placing recipients under arbitrary gatekeeping.

## Proposal

Explore a **floor of effective access** to housing, safe water, adequate food, education and other essentials, without assuming that identical monetary transfers supply equal opportunities. Compare universal service guarantees, direct provision, cooperative provision, individual entitlements and additional supports proportionate to need. People should be able to contest eligibility and provision decisions. Decisions about what counts as a need must involve affected people and allow revision. Connect to [unconditional income](../0008-unconditional-income/en.md), [land and housing](../0011-collective-land-housing/en.md) and [emancipation](../0002-emancipation/en.md).

## Alternatives

Universal basic services; guaranteed minimum income; targeted benefits; social insurance; rights-based public provisioning; commons-based and cooperative schemes; combinations.

## Criticism and limitations

Universal commitments may exceed available resources; subjective need assessments can produce surveillance, stigma or administrative domination. Scarcity and ecological limits require difficult allocation choices. Collective definitions of a 'dignified life' must not impose lifestyles.

## Evaluation

Examine actual access, unmet needs, treatment of disability, dignity in service delivery, barriers to appeal, affordability, ecological sustainability, and differences between formal equality and effective equality.

## Proposed Manifesto Entry

Anarchopoeia examines ways to ensure everyone can effectively access the conditions of a dignified life, including adequate shelter, food, safe water, education and opportunities to act. An equitable system must account for differences in individual situations and needs, ensure accessible remedies and avoid turning essential services into instruments of arbitrary control.

## Research

The [historical founding principles](https://github.com/Nirgalz/Anarchopeia/blob/0d8963af515be54957f767531017aff3d6881955/txt/principes%20fondateurs.txt) use the language of inalienable rights; the [older text on potentials and equity](https://github.com/Nirgalz/Anarchopeia/blob/0d8963af515be54957f767531017aff3d6881955/txt/puissance%20pouvoir%20et%20resolution.txt) argues that equal treatment may require unequal assistance. Separate legal rights, ethical aspirations and practical guarantees. Examine universal versus needs-sensitive allocation, the cost of social infrastructure and fair procedures for resolving conflicts among legitimate needs.
