**Draft status:** exploratory proposal derived from historical notes. Research leads do not constitute verified evidence or an approved manifesto position.

## Problem

Participants may lack usable information about available skills, unmet needs, shared resources and the interdependence of economic activities. The [historical draft](../../txt/ecopolitique.txt) suggests a digital network of offers and requests called **CAN/NEED**.

## Objectives

Evaluate an interoperable, federated information infrastructure for representing needs, offers, skills and resources across scales while respecting participants' privacy, consent and the right not to disclose personal data.

## Proposal

Study a voluntary protocol where individuals, cooperatives and communities may describe **needs (NEED)** and **potential contributions or available resources (CAN)**. Software services could match compatible needs and offers, highlight unmet demand, and optionally share **aggregated** information with wider communities. Distinguish the semantic data model from dynamic price-setting: reported demand must not automatically determine moral worth or access to care. Provide correction, withdrawal, access-control and independent auditing mechanisms. **No P2P protocol, cryptocurrency, algorithm or price formula is adopted.** Related proposals: [economic coordination](../0024-economic-coordination/en.md), [productive resilience](../0013-productive-resilience/en.md) and [critical public information](../0025-deliberation-information/en.md).

## Alternatives

Local public registries; cooperative directories; open-data platforms; federated databases; privacy-preserving data exchanges; non-digital community assemblies.

## Criticism and limitations

Expressed preferences do not equal actual needs; low-literacy and unconnected people may be undercounted. Sensitive data could reveal disability, poverty, location or political beliefs. Matching algorithms may concentrate platform power, produce harmful rankings or enable surveillance.

## Evaluation

Test coverage, underrepresentation, ability to remove data, security, federation without central ownership, algorithmic contestability, data minimization and usefulness for planning under real conditions.

## Proposed Manifesto Entry

Anarchopoeia explores federated tools that allow individuals and communities to describe needs, available resources and possible contributions, and to coordinate responses without concentrating information power. Such tools must be accessible, privacy-preserving, transparent about their limitations and controllable by the people whose information they use.

## Research

The [economic-political notes](../../txt/ecopolitique.txt) outline a distributed database, matching of CAN and NEED entries, and progressive aggregation from households to larger territorial networks. The [cognitive notes](../../txt/outils%20sémantiques%20et%20cognitifs.txt) use the same labels as broad semantic metaphors. These are **not validated technical specifications**; establish a precise schema, distinguish self-reported needs from inferred needs, and examine alternatives to data collection.
