**Draft status:** exploratory proposal derived from historical notes. Research leads do not constitute verified evidence or an approved manifesto position.

## Problem

Control of financing is often concentrated among institutions and holders of capital. The [economic-political notes](https://github.com/Nirgalz/Anarchopeia/blob/0d8963af515be54957f767531017aff3d6881955/txt/ecopolitique.txt) sketch a distinct *investment currency* that would distribute a share of investment-allocation authority equally among members of a community.

## Objectives

Compare mechanisms for equal access to **decision rights over collective investment**, independently of private wealth, without treating simple numerical equality as sufficient for substantive fairness.

## Proposal

Examine regularly allocated **investment credits** that every eligible participant can assign to public or cooperative projects. These units would be conceptually distinct from everyday **consumption money** and would not be freely convertible into personal purchasing power. A project may receive one-off allocations or recurring commitments. The proposed **non-transferability** principle needs to be reconciled with the older suggestion that people may delegate administration of allocations to a trusted party under transparent, revocable rules. Determine whether unallocated credits expire, are pooled, or change the relative weight of others' credits; there is no approved design. Connect to [collective financing](../0015-collective-activity-financing/en.md) and [distributed monetary creation](../0007-distributed-money-creation/en.md).

## Alternatives

Participatory budgeting with ordinary currency; cooperative investment funds; equal voting on a shared public budget; conventional taxation and grants; community bonds.

## Criticism and limitations

Equal credits need not translate into equal voice when information and project-promotion resources are unequal. Popularity may displace necessity, minority projects, preventive maintenance and long-term research. Future commitments can create liabilities. Transferable credits may be bought or captured; non-transferability may restrict legitimate support arrangements.

## Evaluation

Assess concentration of actual allocation influence, diversity of funded projects, access for minorities, long-term commitments, revocability of delegated administration, economic feasibility and accountability for non-delivery.

## Proposed Manifesto Entry

Anarchopoeia examines equal civic rights to direct a share of collective investment toward publicly proposed projects. These rights could take the form of non-exchangeable investment credits, distinct from personal consumption money, assigned individually under transparent rules. Funding decisions should remain accountable and protect needs that cannot be measured solely by popularity.

## Research

The [historical economic-political draft](https://github.com/Nirgalz/Anarchopeia/blob/0d8963af515be54957f767531017aff3d6881955/txt/ecopolitique.txt) describes periodic equal credits, non-storage, subscriptions for multi-year projects, and optional delegation of credit administration. Model their accounting, legal status, enforceability and interactions with other economic resources. Equal credits are a **hypothesis**, not an existing currency design validated by evidence.
