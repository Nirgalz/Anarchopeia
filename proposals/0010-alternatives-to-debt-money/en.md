**Editorial note:** This proposal records one option drawn from an earlier article. It is open to revision, not a collectively endorsed conclusion.

## Problem

The article proposes making 'debt money' obsolete, but the term does not identify one unique mechanism. Credit, debt, money and monetary issuance must be distinguished.

## Objectives

Compare the monetary and financial consequences of systems that rely less on credit-based creation of means of payment.

## Proposal

Investigate alternatives to issuance associated with bank lending, including direct public issuance, mutual-credit accounting and plural monetary systems. Avoid claiming that all credit or debt can or should disappear.

## Alternatives

Reformed banking, public banking, full-reserve proposals, mutual credit, complementary currencies and hybrids.

## Criticism and limitations

Eliminating a specific money-creation mechanism may shift financing risks or create credit shortages. There is no automatic equivalence between debt and exploitation.

## Evaluation

Analyze financing availability, systemic stability, governance, distribution of seigniorage or monetary benefits, and transition constraints.

## Proposed Manifesto Entry

Anarchopoeia investigates alternatives to monetary creation through bank credit, with attention to how means of payment are issued, how productive activities are financed and who controls those decisions. Competing models must be evaluated for their stability, accessibility and impact on domination.

## Research

Establish a precise glossary for money, bank deposits, credit and debt; document actual institutional arrangements; study historical and proposed alternatives.

### Historical notes to investigate — not adopted

The [economic-political notes](https://github.com/Nirgalz/Anarchopeia/blob/0d8963af515be54957f767531017aff3d6881955/txt/ecopolitique.txt) distinguish credit-based money creation from a possible **advance against future voluntary allocations**, through project subscriptions or commitments. This might still create enforceable obligations and financial risk; it should **not** be presented as eliminating every form of debt. Clarify unit of account, liability, default, right to revoke pledges, and consistency with the proposal on investment rights.
