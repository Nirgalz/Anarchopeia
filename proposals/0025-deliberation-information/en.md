**Draft:** Research proposal extracted from historical notes, not an approved position.

## Problem

Public decisions can be distorted by manipulative communication, identification of ideas with personalities, vague categories, unequal access to evidence and false certainty. Several [historic semantic notes](https://github.com/Nirgalz/Anarchopeia/blob/0d8963af515be54957f767531017aff3d6881955/txt/outils%20sémantiques%20et%20cognitifs.txt) explore these risks, but also contain polemical generalizations that must not become orthodoxy.

## Objectives

Develop deliberative practices that encourage clarity, informed disagreement, critical scrutiny of evidence and accountable communication, without licensing a central institution to define the only acceptable beliefs.

## Proposal

Explore a framework for public proposal discussions: clarify the meaning and scope of contested terms, **separate persons from arguments**, distinguish values from empirical claims and causal explanations, display sources and uncertainty, allow dissent and revision, and prevent harassment or manipulative pressure. Support people with different levels of expertise, literacy and language access. Acknowledge that categories and stories aid communication but can obscure individual differences; no category should be treated as determining the beliefs or intentions of every member. Examine information provenance and the influence of publicity and political messaging without equating all persuasion with unlawful manipulation. See [emancipatory education](../0018-emancipatory-education/en.md) and [revocable delegated democracy](../0020-revocable-delegated-democracy/en.md).

## Alternatives

Unstructured debate; expert-only review; deliberative juries; open peer review; fact-checking with appeals; facilitated pluralist assemblies; combinations.

## Criticism and limitations

Rules about sound reasoning can themselves become tools of exclusion or censorship. Demands for evidence must not erase lived experience or disadvantage those without academic credentials. Emotional narratives are not automatically invalid, and no arbiter is perfectly neutral.

## Evaluation

Examine access for dissenting minorities, attribution, source transparency, quality of corrections, clarity of disagreements, appeal against moderation, distribution of speaking time and whether participants can change decisions based on new evidence.

## Proposed Manifesto Entry

Anarchopoeia seeks public deliberation that values arguments over personal allegiance, clarifies contested words, acknowledges uncertainty and keeps conclusions open to revision. Participants should have access to diverse information and meaningful opportunities to challenge claims and decisions without being compelled to share a prescribed worldview.

## Research

The [critique of representation and identification](https://github.com/Nirgalz/Anarchopeia/blob/0d8963af515be54957f767531017aff3d6881955/txt/critiques.txt) warns against confusing political groups with homogeneous entities and ideas with their representatives. The [cognitive notes](https://github.com/Nirgalz/Anarchopeia/blob/0d8963af515be54957f767531017aff3d6881955/txt/outils%20sémantiques%20et%20cognitifs.txt) discuss abstraction, categorization, language, uncertainty and the risks of emotionally charged examples; the [moral essay](https://github.com/Nirgalz/Anarchopeia/blob/0d8963af515be54957f767531017aff3d6881955/txt/morale.txt) contains **inconsistent** generalizations about beliefs and religions. Treat these as prompts for comparative research, not empirically proven psychological classifications or reasons to police beliefs. Operationalize fair source review, appeals, multilingual accessibility and separation of empirical evidence from normative disagreement.
