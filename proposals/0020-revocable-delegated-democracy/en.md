**Draft status:** exploratory proposal derived from historical notes. Research leads do not constitute verified evidence or an approved manifesto position.

## Problem

When political decisions are primarily mediated by party representatives, citizens may have limited influence over specific measures and the public debate may focus on personalities instead of arguments. The [historical notes](../../txt/ecopolitique.txt) sketch an alternative, but neither equal formal votes nor an online platform guarantees effective participation.

## Objectives

Explore accessible, revisable forms of citizen initiative and decision-making that allow **direct voting and issue-specific delegation** while limiting concentration of power.

## Proposal

Study a multi-level procedure in which affected people can submit, discuss, revise and compare policy proposals; eligible participants vote directly **or voluntarily delegate a vote by issue to a person they trust**. Delegations should be revocable and contestable, with explicit rules for transparency, privacy, conflicts of interest and protection from coercion. Specify the political scale and membership for each decision. The older notes mention monthly cycles and a threshold of 2% support: these are **examples to test, not adopted parameters**. Their suggestion that delegate votes be public while ordinary votes remain secret requires serious privacy and coercion analysis. Related proposals: [distribution of powers](../0006-distributed-power/en.md) and [experimental transition](../0019-proto-anarchic-experimentation/en.md).

## Alternatives

Representative elections with mandatory terms; direct-democracy ballots; sortition-based citizen assemblies; liquid democracy; deliberative panels combined with referendums.

## Criticism and limitations

Majority rule can threaten minorities; frequent votes may favor better-resourced participants; delegated voting can concentrate power or reveal private political choices. Digital exclusion, agenda setting, disinformation and rushed decisions require safeguards.

## Evaluation

Compare effective inclusion, informed deliberation, responsiveness, inequality of delegated influence, verifiable tallying, privacy, independence of appeals and protections for fundamental rights. Test whether a delegation can be revoked in practice before the vote closes.

## Proposed Manifesto Entry

Citizens should be able to initiate and debate public decisions and participate directly in voting or voluntarily delegate their vote on particular issues, under transparent and revocable rules. Decision-making procedures must protect equal political participation, privacy, minority rights and effective opportunities to challenge the exercise of delegated authority.

## Research

The [historical economic-political notes](../../txt/ecopolitique.txt) describe staged legislative proposals, social discussion, potential endorsement thresholds and recurring votes. The [political critique](../../txt/critiques.txt) questions identification with representatives rather than examination of ideas. Develop legal and technical models without assuming voting frequency, thresholds, delegate-publicity rules or technological feasibility.
