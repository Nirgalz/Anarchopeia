# Proposals

Specific institutional or practical options belong here once they can be described and challenged. **Nothing is adopted by default.**

## Suggested proposal record

- **Title and status:** exploring / under review / provisionally included / rejected / superseded.
- **Problem and intended outcomes.**
- **Mechanism and responsibilities.**
- **Alternatives and evidence.**
- **Affected people, objections, risks, and tradeoffs.**
- **Conditions, measures, and reversal or exit options.**
- **Related Issues, PRs, research, decisions, and translations.**

Use identical slugs in every language. Proposals may be revised or withdrawn in response to new arguments or evidence.
