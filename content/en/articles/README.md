# Articles

This directory will host readable, sourced articles developed from research. **No articles have yet been approved or migrated.**

## Suggested editorial outline

1. A concrete problem or contradiction.
2. What is known and what remains uncertain.
3. Competing explanations and alternative proposals.
4. Criticism, unintended effects, and tradeoffs.
5. Possibilities for experiment or further investigation.
6. Open questions and links to research, evidence, and proposals.

An article can end with a provisional conclusion or unresolved questions; it need not defend a predetermined answer.
