## Standard of inquiry

Define the question; distinguish facts, values, hypotheses, and proposed mechanisms; examine competing explanations, possible harm, and counterarguments; identify evidence needed and options for testing.

Use [sources](../sources/README.md) and [proposals](../proposals/README.md) as the inquiry develops. The existing [`txt/` notes](../../../txt/) are reserved for later review, not automatically incorporated.
