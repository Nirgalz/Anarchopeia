# Unconditional income

**Status:** preliminary research questions. No institutional model adopted.

## Central question

Which kinds of unconditional material security are feasible and under what conditions?

## Topics to investigate

Compare cash income, universal services, and hybrid arrangements; examine their financing and distribution.

## Critical questions

How might these options affect housing, prices, unpaid care, work, and access to services?

## Next steps

Gather credible, attributable sources and counterevidence. Clarify mechanisms and their possible consequences. Record objections, uncertainty, and affected people. Link concrete options to [proposals](../proposals/README.md), and document important decisions before amending the [manifesto](../manifesto.md).
