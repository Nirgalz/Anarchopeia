# Money and monetary design

**Status:** preliminary research questions. No institutional model adopted.

## Central question

Could monetary rules improve provision and exchange while reducing harmful concentration?

## Topics to investigate

Examine money creation, debt, circulation, monetary decay, redemption, and governance; distinguish money from material wealth.

## Critical questions

Could wealth accumulation shift to non-monetary assets? What about prices, saving, and long-term investment?

## Next steps

Gather credible, attributable sources and counterevidence. Clarify mechanisms and their possible consequences. Record objections, uncertainty, and affected people. Link concrete options to [proposals](../proposals/README.md), and document important decisions before amending the [manifesto](../manifesto.md).
