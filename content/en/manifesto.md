# Living manifesto — working outline

**Status: draft framework; no political program adopted.**

Anarchopeia aims to create an open, revisable manifesto through research, debate, proposals, and evaluation of experiments. This page outlines the inquiry; it does not imply agreement on any particular institution.

## Questions

1. How might institutions reduce domination and concentrated decision-making?
2. How could material security, autonomy, and meaningful participation be supported?
3. Which models of ownership, money, production, and ecological resource management merit investigation?
4. How could education help people understand, question, and transform shared institutions?
5. What would an accountable, gradual, evidence-informed transition look like?
6. How could decisions remain transparent, revisable, and open to dissent?

## Method

Distinguish **values, factual claims, causal hypotheses, and institutional proposals**. Investigate competing models and unintended consequences. Record objections and reasons for decisions. Revise even the foundational assumptions when arguments or evidence warrant it.

## Current status

An initial [discussion paper on aims, emancipation, and non-domination](foundations/aims-emancipation-and-non-domination.md) documents the initiator's current orientation and unresolved questions. No detailed political, monetary, property, or transition model has been adopted here. The [research index](research/README.md), [proposals](proposals/README.md), and [decision log](decisions/README.md) will inform subsequent revisions.
