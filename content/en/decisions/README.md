# Decisions and revisions

This directory will document consequential editorial and governance decisions. **No past collective decisions have been reconstructed or invented.**

## Suggested record

- Date, identifier, and issue under consideration.
- Options evaluated and arguments for and against.
- Relevant evidence and uncertainties.
- Who made the editorial decision and under what authority.
- Result and affected files.
- Dissent, remaining questions, and conditions for reopening.
- Related Issue, PR, and translations.

**Inclusion in a current draft is not collective consensus.** Decisions remain open to reconsideration.
