# Glossary — provisional definitions

These are editorial aids, **not established theoretical conclusions**.

- **Living manifesto:** a versioned, revisable synthesis of arguments, proposals, uncertainties, and disagreements.
- **Proposal:** a specific option to examine; publication does not imply adoption.
- **Hypothesis:** a claim that could be questioned or tested using evidence.
- **Domination:** a contested concept concerning uncontrolled or unaccountable power over others; differing definitions deserve investigation.
- **Distributed governance:** allocation of decision authority among people or groups, with explicit responsibilities and safeguards; distribution alone does not ensure fairness.
- **Proto-anarchist / proto-anarchic:** provisional expressions for an evolving transitional idea; no settled project definition yet exists.

Open an Issue for alternative definitions, conceptual comparisons, or contested interpretations.
