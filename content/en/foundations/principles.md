# Working principles — open to revision

These are **suggested standards for inquiry**, not a collectively ratified political charter.

- **Fallibility:** any analysis, proposal, or principle may be challenged with reasons.
- **Traceability:** preserve the rationale for important changes and unresolved objections.
- **Pluralism:** represent opposing arguments fairly, without treating all claims as equally evidenced.
- **Accessibility:** write for non-specialists and support translation.
- **Accountability:** distinguish declared ideals from actual powers and responsibilities.
- **Non-domination in practice:** examine whether project procedures reproduce undue concentration of decision-making.

**Open question:** What principles, if any, should become binding, and how might that status be gained or revised?
