# Purpose — draft

Explore institutions that might support dignity, autonomy, cooperation, ecological sustainability, and shared accountability. Investigate the tensions and tradeoffs between these goals instead of assuming they always align.

The **research purpose** is to formulate, examine, compare, and revise institutional alternatives. The **editorial purpose** is to make this inquiry intelligible and accessible.

## Questions still open

- How are these goals defined, and by whom?
- How can individual freedom and collective obligations coexist?
- Which observations might show that a proposal has failed or caused harm?
- How can people who reject the starting assumptions participate meaningfully?
